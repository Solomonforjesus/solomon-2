Explore Scripture together through ten levels, from Creation and familiar Bible stories to the life of Jesus, the Gospel, and Christian living. Play on your own, take turns as a family, or discuss the answers with a church group.

  1. Bible Beginnings — Creation, Noah, early Bible stories
  2. Heroes of Faith — Moses, David, Daniel, Jonah and other familiar Bible people
  3. Amazing Bible Events — Red Sea, Jericho, Goliath, Jonah and more
  4. Kings, Prophets & Promises — Solomon, Elijah, Isaiah and God's promises

  6. Walking with Jesus — Faith, prayer, compassion and forgiveness

  8. The Gospel — Sin, grace, faith, salvation and forgiveness
  9. Living for Jesus — Holy Spirit, Scripture, prayer and Christian life
  10. Kingdom Champion — Put your whole Bible journey together

Try three sample questions

Choose a question to reveal its answer and Scripture reference.

Who created the heavens and the earth?

God. God is the Creator of the heavens, the earth, and everything in them.

Read together: Genesis 1:1

Moses was placed in a basket when he was a baby. Where was the basket placed?

In a river. Moses' mother placed him in a basket among the reeds by the river, where Pharaoh's daughter found him.

Read together: Exodus 2:3-5

Where was Jesus born?

Bethlehem. Jesus was born in Bethlehem, just as Scripture had foretold.

Read together: Luke 2:4-7
