Before You Step Into the Day

By Gary Dearing

Before you step into the day, spend a moment with the Lord. Ask Him to clothe you in His whole armor—to steady you in truth, guard your heart, and prepare your feet to carry His peace. Take up faith against the enemy’s lies, remember whose you are, and keep His Word close. Pray as you go.

Ask the Holy Spirit to grow His fruit in you: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. Remember that fruit when someone interrupts your plans or tests your patience. That moment may be where He invites you to grow.

And ask Jesus to help you see the person before you. There may be hurt beneath the irritation, fear behind the anger, or loneliness behind the smile. Listen. Be gentle. Speak truth with love.

The enemy seeks to deceive, distract, and destroy. He would gladly turn our wounds against one another. Stay close to Jesus and stand in the strength He provides. The person before you is someone to love.

Lord, dress me for the day, grow Your fruit in me, and help me treat each person as You would. Amen.

Galatians 5:16–25; Ephesians 6:10–18
